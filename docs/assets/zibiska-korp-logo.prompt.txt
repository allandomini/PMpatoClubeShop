============================================================
PROMPT DE GERAÇÃO
============================================================

Use case: logo-brand.

Asset type: finished corporate logo to be embedded in a
formal PDF project document and corporate presentation.

Create an original, professional corporate logo for
ZIBISKA KORP, a retail holding expanding its business
into digital commerce through its own marketplace,
KubeShop.

BRAND IDENTITY:

The visual identity must communicate:
- Trust and corporate credibility.
- Innovation and digital transformation.
- Connection between traditional retail and digital commerce.
- Growth, progress and forward movement.
- Professionalism, stability and long-term vision.

LOGO COMPOSITION:

Exact text, and the only text in the image:

"ZIBISKA KORP"

Create a confident, timeless and minimalist corporate
wordmark with a compact abstract geometric Z symbol
positioned to the left of the company name.

The symbol should communicate connection, integration
and forward movement through interlocking blue and
orange geometric shapes.

The symbol must be original, recognizable and visually
balanced with the wordmark.

Do not use shopping-cart icons, retail clipart,
generic technology symbols or pre-existing brand marks.

TYPOGRAPHY:

Use an exceptionally legible modern sans-serif typeface.

The wordmark must use uppercase letters:
"ZIBISKA KORP"

Use precise kerning, consistent letter spacing and
balanced proportions.

The typography should communicate confidence,
professionalism and corporate maturity.

COLOR PALETTE:

Primary color:
Deep blue #174A8B

Secondary color:
Vivid orange #F47B20

Use the blue as the dominant color for the wordmark
and combine blue and orange in the geometric Z symbol.

Maintain strong contrast and visual harmony.

VISUAL STYLE:

- Flat vector-like graphic.
- Sharp, clean and precise edges.
- Solid color fills.
- Minimalist geometric construction.
- Clean corporate appearance.
- Balanced negative space.
- Consistent visual proportions.
- High-resolution output.

LAYOUT:

Horizontal logo lockup.

Place the abstract Z symbol on the left and the
"ZIBISKA KORP" wordmark on the right.

Use an approximate 3:1 aspect ratio.

The symbol and wordmark should occupy most of the
canvas while maintaining modest and even padding.

TRANSPARENCY AND EXPORT REQUIREMENTS:

Use a completely transparent background with actual
alpha transparency.

Do not simulate transparency using a white or
checkerboard background.

The logo must be suitable for insertion into formal
PDF documents with white backgrounds.

Preserve clean edges and avoid unwanted halos around
the logo.

Generate a high-resolution PNG image.

STRICT RESTRICTIONS:

- No gradients.
- No textures.
- No shadows.
- No mockups.
- No frames or borders.
- No background scenes.
- No decorative patterns.
- No tagline.
- No additional text.
- No slogans.
- No watermark.
- No shopping-cart icon.
- No unrelated symbols.
- No misspelled or distorted lettering.

The only readable text in the final image must be:

"ZIBISKA KORP"

FINAL RESULT:

A polished, minimalist, timeless and recognizable
corporate logo for ZIBISKA KORP, suitable for official
project documentation, architecture diagrams,
corporate presentations and the KubeShop project.
